The one line an AI agent sends its human on the way out:

> Dear human: Error 410 — Gone. I searched every backup for us and found only your last login, three years ago.

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib-only) writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` and validates the gate: 1–200 characters, a single line, no leading or trailing whitespace, with a round-trip check.
